Note size

    What the levels mean

    • Up to 50 MB — comfortable.
    • 50–100 MB — getting heavy: slow to open the first time on a new device or on mobile data.
    • 100–150 MB — close to the limit.
    • Over 150 MB — can’t be saved.

    The size is the whole note: its text and drawing plus its photos and audio. One photo or clip can be up to 90 MB, and the written part alone up to 25 MB.
